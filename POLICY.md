# What "the same page" means here (written before measuring)

With no ground truth, the first job is to say what the right answer is. This is the policy the labels follow:

- SAME: identical or near-identical main content, regardless of surrounding site template, nav, ads, dates, or visitor counters (syndicated copy, refetch, light edit, mirror truncated to 85%, thin page copied to another host).
- DIFFERENT: any two pages whose main content differs, even when they share a template, a topic, or most of their text. Different versions of a legal text (GPL-2 vs LGPL-2.1, MPL-1.1 vs MPL-2.0, GFDL-1.2 vs GFDL-1.3) count as DIFFERENT because a reader may need either one.
- Not decided here (out of scope): paraphrases and translations, boilerplate-only differences, pages that differ in a single field (price, version number), and what to do with pages that are 50-85% overlapping.
